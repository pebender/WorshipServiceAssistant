Hide Button

The 'Hide' button one the Worship Service Assistant command bar toggles the active presentation's slide show between hidden and shown. If the active presentation does not have a slide show, then the 'Hide' button has no effect.

The 'Hide' button is designed to be used during the worship service. It allows the operator to toggle the slide show between hidden and shown. Simply click the 'Hide' button.

It should be noted that this button's behavior is slightly different from the Navigator's 'Hide/Show' button. After clicking this button, the presentation's slide show window is the active window. After clicking the Navigator's 'Hide/Show' button, the presentation's window is the active window.
