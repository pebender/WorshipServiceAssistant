Banner Disable/Enable Button

Clicking the 'Banner Disable/Enable' button in the 'Banner Configuration' frame disables or enables the banner. When the banner is enabled, the presentation is reduced in size to make room for the banner above the presentation. The CONTROL+D and CONTROL+E key combinations have the same effect as the 'Disable/Enable' button.

The 'Banner Disable/Enable' button is designed to be used at the start of the worship service. If the operator may need to display banner text during the service, then the operator should enable the banner before the service starts. Otherwise, the operator can disable (the default) before the service starts. The banner can also be enabled or disabled during the worship service. However, because it does resize the presentation it can be somewhat disruptive unless the presentation is hidden. Simply click the 'Banner Disable/Enable' button or use the CONTROL+D or CONTROL+E key combinations.
