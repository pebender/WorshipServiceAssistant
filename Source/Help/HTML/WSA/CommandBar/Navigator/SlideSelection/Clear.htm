Clear Button

Clicking the 'Clear' button in the Slide Selection frame in the Navigator frame clears the slide number filter and slide title filter, causing all slides in the active presentation to appear in the 'List' display. The DELETE key has the same effect as the 'Clear' button.

The 'Clear' button is designed to be used during the worship service. Simply click the 'Clear' button or use the DELETE key.
