Title Display

The 'Title' display in the Slide Selection frame in the Navigator frame shows the slide title filter being applied to the 'List' display.

The slide title filter is designed to be used during the worship service. Simply type alphanumeric characters and they will appear in the 'Title' display. Only the slides with titles starting with the text in the 'Title' display will appear in the 'List' display. The BACKSPACE key can be used to clear the slide title filter one character at a time. The DELETE key can be used to clear the entire slide title filter.
