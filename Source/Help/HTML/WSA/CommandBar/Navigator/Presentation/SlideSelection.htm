Slide Selection Frame

The 'Slide Selection' frame on the 'Presentation' page of the Navigator allows you to quickly find and select a slide by filtering out unwanted slides. Slides can be filtered by either slide number or slide title.

If the filter text is numeric, then the Navigator assumes that the filter is a slide number. When the Navigator is assuming that the filter is a slide number, the Navigator places the filter text in the 'Slide Selection' 'Number' display. Only the slide with the corresponding slide number will appear in the 'List' display.

If the filter text is not numeric, then the Navigator assumes that the filter is the beginning of a slide title. When the Navigator is assuming that the filter is a slide title, then Navigator places the filter text in the 'Slide Selection' 'Title' display. Only slides that have titles beginning with the filter text will appear in the 'Slide Selection' 'List' display. When filtering by title, the Navigator window attempts to be somewhat forgiving. Capitalization, punctuation and white space are not considered when filtering. For example, the filter "lord i lift" will match titles beginning with "Lord, I lift".
