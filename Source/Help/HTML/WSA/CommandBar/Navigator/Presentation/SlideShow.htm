Slide Show Frame

The 'Slide Show' frame on the 'Presentation' page of the Navigator allows you to easily control the most used slide show functions.

The 'Load' button allows you to display the slide selected in the 'List' display in the 'Slide Selection' frame.

The 'Run' button, 'Pause' button and 'Hide/Show' button allow you to run, pause and hide/show the slide show respectively.

The 'Prev Effect' button and 'Next Effect' button allow you to show the previous and next slide show effect respectively.
